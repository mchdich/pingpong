How to play

Your computer shows the original Poddle court. Your iPhone or Android phone swings the paddle in real time. The original Auto movement runs your player to the ball.

Start a match

  1. Open pingpong.mehdi.ch on your computer or TV browser, press Play, and enter your name.
  2. Pick 1v1 for two players or 2v2 for four. Quick play finds an open court; Create court gives you a link and code to share.
  3. Each player opens the shared link on their own screen and pairs their own phone.
  4. Scan the phone QR code, or open pingpong.mehdi.ch/pad on the phone and type its P-code. Tap Start and allow motion access.

Calibrate your phone

  1. Hold it like a paddle handle: edge up, screen sideways, top end pointing toward the computer.
  2. Keep still until the first progress bar fills.
  3. Tip the phone’s top end upward, then hold it there until the second bar fills.
  4. Return to a comfortable grip. Turn and swing the phone to control the paddle.

Press C on the computer, or hold Calibrate again on the phone, to repeat both steps. R or the phone’s Recentre button resets the resting direction.

Hit with control

Your player runs to meet the ball. Watch it approach and time your swing. Turning the phone turns the paddle; use a normal forehand, backhand, or scoop just as in Poddle. A forehand-side ball needs a forehand, and a backhand-side ball needs a backhand. Choose Right or Left under Paddle hand in Settings.

Soft, medium, and hard swings produce different pace, trails, and sounds. A gentle hit from the baseline may not clear the net; a soft dink close to the net can still work. Too hard can go long. Your swing direction aims the ball, and pointing left or right adjusts the target. A take-back does not lock every shot to one side. Sideways targets stay near the safe edge and never cause out faults. Roll your wrist or curve the swing to add spin.

Spikes and ultimates

Chop downward through a high ball close to the net to spike it. Your player still needs to reach the ball on the correct side. A soft spike can hit the net; a hard one can go long.

Land five orange-strength hits in a row to fill your Critical streak counter. Orange is the second-hardest strength, below red. A weaker hit, overpowered hit, or missed point breaks an unfinished streak. At 5/5, choose Swing speedup for faster, stronger team swings or Slow opponents for slower, weaker swings and greatly reduced movement. A slowed player cannot run to a distant ball in time. Each lasts six seconds.

The paddle rotates with your phone: screen facing up means the paddle face faces up. Confirmed hits vibrate in supported browsers, with stronger feedback for harder swings. iPhone browsers currently cannot provide web vibration.

Court rules

  • First to 11 points, win by two. Every rally scores.
  • Serve by swinging at the waiting ball. It must clear the net and land before the opponent’s baseline.
  • A shot that lands on the opponent’s side before their baseline wins if they cannot return it before its second bounce. You can also volley. A net ball or one landing long loses the point. Going left or right does not cause an out fault.
  • In 2v2, the player closest to the incoming ball runs to meet it. Either teammate can return a ball within reach. If both swing at the same reachable ball at the same time, both miss. The highlighted serving player starts the rally.

Connection or motion problems

Keep the phone page open and its screen awake. On iPhone, choose Allow when motion access is requested. On Android, use a browser with motion sensors enabled, such as Chrome.

If the phone says Looking for your computer, check that the computer’s game tab is open and that its P-code matches. Recalibrate after changing grip or turning to face a different screen.
