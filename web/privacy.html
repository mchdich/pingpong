Privacy Policy

Last updated October 6, 2026

Information used to play

The phone sends its orientation, rotation rate, acceleration, and timestamps through the game server to the computer tab paired with its P-code. The computer uses these samples to move and swing the paddle. The server receives the paddle position, orientation, and swings to simulate the match. Recent paddle motion and ball positions are held in memory for contact timing and fair-play checks.

Display names, court codes, connection status, scores, and paddle movements are shared with the other players and spectators on your court. Choose a display name you are comfortable with other people seeing. Rooms and live game state are held in server memory.

This phone game does not request a webcam or an AirPod helper connection. iPhone asks for motion permission after you tap Start. The phone may request permission to keep its screen awake. If you choose Find my speakers in Settings, the browser may ask for microphone permission to name audio output devices; the game immediately stops the temporary stream.

Optional statistics and accounts

The original game includes statistics and account features. Where enabled, statistics are linked to a random device identifier or a signed-in account. Sign-in is not required to play. The game retains an account identifier from Google, rather than its email address, name, or picture. Sign-in uses protected cookies to maintain the requested session.

You can use Download a copy or Delete my data in the original statistics and privacy controls where those features are enabled. Clearing browser data removes local settings and calibration; it does not itself erase statistics already saved on the server.

Hosting and retention

Fly.io hosts the game and handles network information such as IP addresses and request details as part of serving the site. Phone and game connections use HTTPS and encrypted WebSockets on the public site. Motion samples are used for live play and are not saved as recordings. Rooms and live connection state disappear when the server restarts or the court is closed.

The game does not include advertising or third-party analytics, sell information, or use game information to train AI. Browser settings and calibration stay on your device until you clear them.
